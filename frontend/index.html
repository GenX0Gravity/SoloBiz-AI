Local-First Architecture & Privacy Guarantees

Zero Cloud Telemetry

SoloBiz AI is engineered specifically so freelancers and small businesses can maintain 100% financial privacy. Unlike cloud-hosted solutions that transmit financial records, client names, and tax documents to third-party APIs, SoloBiz AI keeps all execution on-device.

Local SQLite Storage
Expenses, invoices, and documents are stored in data/solobiz.db on your local hard drive. Fully queryable with parameterized SQL.
Local Document Files
Uploaded receipt PDFs and images reside in your local uploads/ folder with SHA-256 deduplication and path sanitization.
Ollama Open-Weight AI
AI reasoning is powered by open-weight models running on your local GPU/CPU. Zero API keys, zero cloud egress, zero subscription fees.
